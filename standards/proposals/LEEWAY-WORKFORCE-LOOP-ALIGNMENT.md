## Strategic mission beginning and ending
**Beginning:** Recover the complete original mission outcome, current checkpoint, original owner and scope, 5WH, source SHA, Standards links, relevant Skill/REA evidence, authority, history, peer notes, acceptance test and recovery plan. A new session or employee is not a new parent mission.

**Ending:** The original observable outcome passes its actual required acceptance contract, independent security/Veritas and receipts where applicable, and the entire journey and eligible learning are preserved. Partial source, fixture or simulation success advances only the proven gate; no false physical completion.

**Blocked states:** Investigate each blocker as a learning event. Hold the particular restricted effect; advance every independent authorized gate. Preserve owner, required decision and checkpoint, and re-enter the same mission through the relevant loop stage.

## Ten stages: workforce and learning requirements
1. **Investigate:** Recover original source/authority, current rules and immutable links, prior cases, worker competencies, constraints and claims.
2. **Diagnose:** Prove actual defect, classify recurring signature and distinguish employee skill from tooling, authority, environment and governance blockers.
3. **Plan:** State full strategic acceptance, tier-specific time budget, role-qualified peers, safe intervention, permitted staffing proposal, test matrix and rollback.
4. **Implement:** Repair canonical owner; apply mentoring and verified Skills; add proportionate guards; preserve existing functioning work.
5. **Test:** Record real, controlled and simulated test scopes separately, with exact environment, inputs, negative cases and observed outputs.
6. **Validate:** Compare actual evidence with declared original acceptance and current Standards/Formula gates; advance legitimate intermediate results.
7. **Repair:** Correct demonstrated causes; examine sibling failures and reusable prevention; keep active missions safe during worker change.
8. **Retest:** Repeat original defect and sibling regressions; learner independently reproduces demonstrated competence without a mentor substituting work.
9. **Verify:** Respect independent Veritas, security and evidence separation; no self-awards or worker-issued identity/authority; validate stage-specific proficiency.
10. **Evidence:** Preserve mission history, exact source links/SHAs, 5WH, rule-to-work proofs, timings, full test/repair cycles, mentorship, pattern prevention, receipts and eligible Learning, plus continuation or final acceptance.

## Workforce operations and earned merit
- **Uniform workmanship:** All human and AI contributors must show pride in correct, reproducible and teachable engineering work; same applicable Standards and evidence, different human employment responsibilities.
- **Team excellence:** Qualified workers help peers attain top badges; verified independent teach-back demonstrates genuine knowledge transfer and merits mentor/collective recognition.
- **Hire or delegate:** Agent Lee PRIME may propose additional specialization or staffing only within existing Workforce/Runtime and Creator-approved identity, permission and capacity limits. The Agent Tablet mirrors authenticated canonical states; it creates no separate registry.
- **94% candidate trigger:** A role-relevant evidence-backed score under 94% triggers a supportive capability review, not automatic termination or privilege removal. Review denominator, assessment quality, actual cause, assistance, independent retest and workload continuity. Human employment decisions require competent human review.
- **Transitions:** Authorized suspension, replacement, retirement or reassignment preserves assignments, leases, effects, recovery and receipts; any unexpected worker departure must not orphan a mission.
- **Timed assignments:** Contractually defined tier and real clock observations inform efficiency only after correctness, mandatory engineering-loop stages and independent verification pass; blockers and protected work are not penalized as slowness.
- **Recognition:** Integration & Recovery; Provenance & Transfer; Autonomous Qualification; Software-As-Iron. Separately evaluate operational outcome, prevention, mentorship and collective performance. Badges are verifiable evidence projections and **never independently confer permissions or trust roots**.
- **Pattern learning:** Normalize behavioral signatures; separate hypotheses from established causes; enforce authorized regression protections and preserve eligible institutional learning. Never treat a failed test as an endpoint.
- **Permanent workers:** Temporary development workers transfer tested methods to sovereign LeeWay workers; qualification requires authorized local execution, independent recovery, disconnect trials and teachability without falsely assuming perfection.
